Kitchen Playground

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Kitchen Playground is a cooking game for children and families, made by Luis Quesada ("we"). This policy covers the Kitchen Playground app for Android and for iPhone / iPad, including its test (beta) versions.

In short: the game collects nothing about you or your child.

  • No personal data. The game does not ask for a name, an e-mail address, an age, a location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it has no accounts or log-in.
  • No internet. The game does not connect to the internet. It sends nothing to us or to anyone else. The only link in the game, Privacy in the menu, opens this page in your web browser.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. There is no advertising, no tracking of any kind and no analytics or crash-reporting service inside the game.
  • No chat, no sharing with strangers. Children cannot talk to anyone or publish anything through the game.
  • What stays on the device. The game remembers its own settings and progress (for example the language, text size and the kitchen as it was left) in the app's private storage on the device. This never leaves the device. A picture of a finished dish is only shown on screen. Deleting the app deletes all of this.
  • Permissions. The game asks for no special permissions (no camera, microphone, location or files).

The app stores (Google Play, Apple App Store / TestFlight) handle downloads and, if you take part in a test, your tester sign-up and any feedback you choose to send. They do this under their own privacy policies; we only receive the anonymous summaries those stores give every developer.

Children's privacy: the game is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families policy and Apple's rules for kids' apps. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to share, sell or delete.

Changes: if this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
